# Studio 47 dovanų kuponas: 3D išpakavimo iš voko promptai

Modelis: **Grok Video 1.5** (Higgsfield; Seedance 2.5 reikalauja „plus“ plano), kupono nuotrauka kaip `image_references`.
Formatas: 9:16 (Reels / TikTok / Stories), 8 s, 720p.

Bendra taisyklė visiems variantams (jau įrašyta kiekviename prompte): kortelė turi atitikti nuotrauką 1:1:
balta kortelė, juodas aštrus „saulės“ ženklas centre, tuščios vardo ir pavardės eilutės, didelis „50“, apačioje `@studio47tattoo`.

## 1. BLACK WAX: juodas vokas su vaško antspaudu

> Ultra-premium 3D product unboxing, vertical 9:16, hyperreal macro cinematography. A matte black heavyweight cotton envelope lies on a dark brushed-steel surface, a glossy black wax seal pressed with the spiky sun emblem from the reference card. Slow dolly-in under a single soft top light with a thin rim light. The wax seal cracks in extreme slow motion, tiny wax fragments drift up, the flap lifts by itself with a satisfying paper sound. The white gift voucher card from the reference image rises out of the envelope, levitates, and rotates slowly to face the camera, casting a soft shadow. Final frame: card perfectly frontal and sharp, exactly as in the reference — black spiky sun logo in the center, blank name and surname lines, big number "50", footer "@studio47tattoo". Keep all text and the logo identical to the reference, no extra text. Shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, deep blacks, crisp whites, luxury fashion-brand aesthetic, ASMR paper and wax sound design.

## 2. ZERO-G: levituojantis vokas ir išardytas vaizdas

> Futuristic zero-gravity 3D unboxing, vertical 9:16, clean white infinite studio void, Apple-keynote-level product render. A minimalist off-white textured envelope floats and slowly spins in mid-air. It unfolds itself origami-style: the flaps peel open one by one and the paper layers separate into an elegant exploded view, hovering in perfect symmetry. From the center, the white gift voucher card from the reference image glides forward toward the camera, while fine black ink particles swirl around it and resolve into the spiky black sun logo printed on the card. Smooth orbiting camera, ending in a locked-off hero shot of the card facing the camera, perfectly readable and identical to the reference — blank name and surname lines, big "50", "@studio47tattoo" at the bottom. Do not invent any extra text. Soft global illumination, gentle reflections on the floor, ultra-sharp 4K detail, airy whoosh and soft chime sound design.

## 3. INK & SKIN: tatuiruočių studijos ritualas

> Cinematic tattoo-studio unboxing, vertical 9:16, moody editorial lighting. On a black leather tattoo-station table next to an idle rotary tattoo machine and small ink caps, a pair of heavily tattooed hands slides a kraft-black envelope toward the camera. The envelope is closed with a black ink-drip seal. The hands break the seal, a single drop of black ink runs down the paper, and they pull out the white gift voucher card from the reference image in one smooth motion. Camera push-in and rack focus from the ink drop to the card. The card is held up to camera, filling the frame: black spiky sun logo in the center, blank name and surname lines, big "50", footer "@studio47tattoo", exactly as in the reference with no extra text. Warm practical lamp light, haze in the air, shallow depth of field, rich contrast, 35mm film look, ASMR sounds of paper, the soft buzz of a tattoo machine in the background.
